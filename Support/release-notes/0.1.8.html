Melatonin 0.1.8

  • Stay online no longer fights your phone's hotspot. When your Mac is on a hotspot whose cellular connection drops for a moment, Melatonin now waits for the phone instead of restarting Wi-Fi, which only knocked the hotspot off.
  • It gives a network time to settle. A network macOS or you just joined is left alone for a minute and a half, and Melatonin never tries to rejoin the network your Mac is already on.
  • No more endless Wi-Fi restarts. If two restarts in a row don't bring the internet back, it stops and leaves Wi-Fi to macOS until something changes, so a long trip with the lid closed shows “Couldn’t reconnect” a couple of times, not every few minutes.
